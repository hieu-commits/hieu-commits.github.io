01 / ENGINEERING PROFILE

I build, analyze, and improve systems.

I bring a systems mindset to technical problems: understand the constraints, choose a sound approach, and make the outcome verifiable.

⌘ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Think clearly.
Build deliberately.

I work from first principles and break complex systems into testable parts. My focus is on clear implementation, structured troubleshooting, documented trade-offs, and results that can be verified.
